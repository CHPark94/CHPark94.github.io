Book description: 
EXHAUSTION DOESN'T HAVE TO BE YOUR NEW NORMAL.
Discover how to conquer burnout and boost your energy with a proven plan for hormone balancing from a leading medical doctor. Ready to go from feeling effing tired to effing fantastic?

누구나 그 중요성을 인지하고 있지만, 꾸준히 관리하기 어려운 '호르몬'에 대해서 의학적인 내용을 일반인이 이해하기 쉽도록 잘 설명하였다. 나의 몸에 대해서 얼마나 잘 아는지가 건강의 비결이라는 느낌이 들었다. 여성분들에게 적극 추천해주고 싶다.

The book does an excellent job of explaining hormones, something everyone recognizes as important but finds hard to keep in balance, in medical terms that the general reader can easily follow. It left me with the sense that the real secret to good health lies in how well we understand our own bodies. I would especially recommend it to women.
